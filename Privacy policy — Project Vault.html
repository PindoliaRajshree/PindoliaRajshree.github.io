Privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

This policy explains what data Project Vault ("the app") handles, why, where it is stored and the choices you have. Project Vault is a personal project developed and operated by Rajshree Pindolia, Bhuj, Kutch, Gujarat, India ("we", "us" in this policy). Contact: pindoliaraji@gmail.com.

In short: you choose what to store; secrets and files are encrypted on your device before upload; we do not sell your data, show ads or use your data to train AI models.

2. Data received from Google (Google Drive)

Connecting Google Drive is optional and is used only by the desktop app's weekly backup feature.

  • Access requested: the drive.file scope, which allows the app to create, read, update and delete only the files and folders it creates itself. The app cannot see or access any other file in your Google Drive. The app also reads your Google account email address to show which account is connected.
  • How we use it: to create a "Project Vault Backups" folder, upload your encrypted backup files to it, download them when you restore, and delete old versions and backups you remove. Nothing else.
  • Where it is stored: backup files are stored in your own Google Drive. Your Google sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer, in its secure credential store. They are not sent to our servers. Backup records in our database contain Google Drive file and folder IDs, but no Google content.
  • Sharing: we do not transfer, sell or share data received from Google with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads it.
  • Limited Use: Project Vault's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: use Backups → Disconnect in the app, or remove Project Vault at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your existing backup files stay in your Drive until you delete them.

3. Where your data is stored

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), in its South Asia (Mumbai) region. Supabase processes this data on our behalf as a service provider.
  • Your Google Drive for backups, if you connect it.
  • Your devices: if you choose "Remember on this device", your encryption key is kept in the device's secure storage (Keychain, Keystore or Credential Manager) until you lock the vault. Display preferences and backup folder locations are kept in the app's local settings.

4. How we protect it

  • Your master password is turned into an encryption key on your device using Argon2id. Data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before upload.
  • Database rules ensure each account can access only its own data.
  • All connections use HTTPS.
  • Copied passwords and keys are cleared from your clipboard after 30 seconds.

Important: because we never have your master password, we cannot recover encrypted data if you forget it.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Your data is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete your account.
  • You can delete items, projects and backups in the app at any time. Deleting a backup removes its files from your Google Drive.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, email us from your account's email address. We will delete it within 30 days.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct and delete your data in the app, and ask us to confirm what we hold, correct it, delete it or stop processing it. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you and raise a grievance with us. Email pindoliaraji@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children

Project Vault is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. For significant changes we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
