Delete your Vault Safe account

Vault Safe by Rajshree Pindolia

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Vault Safe on any device (web, Windows or Android) and sign in.
  2. Open the menu (⋮) at the top right and choose Security & updates.
  3. Choose Delete account, type the confirmation word shown and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email (if you can't use the app)

Email pindoliaraji@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Vault Safe account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email. For your security, we can only act on requests sent from the account's own email address.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in details)
  • All projects, credentials, console logins, API keys and .env contents, key files, app builds, links, notes and uploaded files
  • Your encryption settings and the records of your backups

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep it.

What is not deleted

  • Backup files in your own Google Drive. They belong to you and stay in the "Vault Safe Backups" folder until you delete that folder in Google Drive. Without your master password they cannot be opened.
  • To also remove Vault Safe's access to Google Drive, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Vault Safe.
